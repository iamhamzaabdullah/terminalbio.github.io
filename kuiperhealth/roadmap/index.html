Kuiper Health roadmap

Prove continuity at one clinic first.

The development plan starts with synthetic records and a narrowly scoped app and card workflow. A clinical pilot would follow only after review and authorization.

From synthetic records to a clinic pilot

Each phase has a test that informs whether the next one should begin. The sequence is planned and depends on technical review and approvals.

  1. 01

    Foundation

    Prove record exchange

    Define the FHIR R4 schema and exercise the Kuiper Chain test environment using synthetic records.

    Evidence to review Schema validation, reliable transactions and a queryable audit trail.

  2. 02

    Product

    Connect app, card and reader

    Prototype mobile identity and QR sharing, then test online record retrieval from an NFC tap.

    Evidence to review A complete sharing flow, recovery tests and security review.

  3. 03

    Clinical evaluation

    Test with one clinic

    Subject to approvals, evaluate a small workflow with clinic staff and patients.

    Evidence to review Retrieval time, record integrity, staff usability and whether the summary helps the handoff.

  4. 04

    Regional evaluation

    Adapt to local workflows

    Use pilot results to decide which integrations and offline workflows warrant further work.

    Evidence to review Local interoperability, governance and reconnection behavior.
