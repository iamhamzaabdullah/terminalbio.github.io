KUIPER HEALTH / ROADMAP

Kuiper Health roadmap

Prove continuity at one clinic first.

The development plan starts with synthetic records and a narrowly scoped app and card workflow. A clinical pilot would follow only after review and authorization.

From prototype to pilot

  1. Foundation

    Validate a minimal FHIR R4 record with synthetic examples. Test the permissioned network and audit trail.

  2. Product

    Prototype patient identity, mobile record sharing and the NFC card. Test recovery, access revocation and reader behavior.

  3. Clinical pilot

    If approved, evaluate record retrieval, data integrity and usability in a limited clinic workflow.

  4. Regional integration

    Use pilot evidence to decide whether to pursue country-specific integrations and offline deployment.

The roadmap presents the planned sequence. Pilot participation, launch dates and integrations depend on evaluation and partner approvals.
