Kuiper Health

Keep the record with the person.

A vision for connected healthcare. Kuiper Health brings together portable health records, blockchain-backed permissions, AI-assisted tools and access by card or phone.

THE VISION

Access to your health information, wherever you receive care.

Kuiper Health proposes shared infrastructure for care across hospitals, local clinics and telehealth. A patient could carry a verified identity, share a selected record with a provider, and see how it was accessed. The ambition is universal access, beginning with a workflow that one clinic can test.

  1. PATIENTTap or scan

    Use the NFC Health Card or a QR code from the Patient App to start a record request.

  2. KUIPERVerify and exchange

    Check the credential and access permission, retrieve the encrypted FHIR record and log the event on Kuiper Chain.

  3. CARE TEAMContinue care

    Open the permitted summary and record the encounter. In a future workflow, reviewed AI tools could help organize the authorized information.

DEVELOPMENT PATH

From concept to connected care

Kuiper Health is at the early concept stage. The phases below show what the project proposes to build and how each phase would inform the next.

  1. 01

    Foundation · Planned

    Prove the exchange

    Validate a minimal FHIR record and test the permissioned ledger using synthetic data.

  2. 02

    Product · Planned

    Connect the app and card

    Prototype digital identity, QR sharing and NFC reader access. Test recovery and access controls.

  3. 03

    Pilot · Subject to approval

    Learn from one clinic

    Measure whether an authorized record can reach the right provider in time to support a handoff.

  4. 04

    Longer term

    Connect more settings

    Evaluate regional interoperability and offline access before expanding toward telehealth, AI and consent-governed research.
