KUIPER HEALTH / MALAYSIA

Malaysia

Connect public and private care.

This proposed pathway examines how Kuiper could complement MySejahtera and make selected records easier to share across clinics, hospitals and virtual care.

Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia · Igor Savelev / Unsplash

Proposed integration

MySejahtera and patient summaries

Start by mapping a FHIR exchange in a development environment. Evaluate whether a concise patient summary can follow referrals across public and private care.

Rural continuity

Explore limited offline access in Sabah, Sarawak and rural Peninsular Malaysia, with local teams defining what information should be available.

Card and virtual care

Explore how an NFC credential and a patient app could connect encounters recorded by participating clinics and telehealth providers.

Evaluation sequence

  1. Validate the exchange

    Check record mapping with synthetic referrals.

  2. Test at the clinic

    Measure retrieval time, summary completeness and staff workload.

  3. Assess later use cases

    Study whether a portable summary could help travelers and whether a clinician-led genomic results workflow is feasible.

A handoff to evaluate

Follow a synthetic referral from a Klinik Kesihatan to a private provider. Test whether the provider can retrieve an authorized summary and whether the follow-up can be shared back. The study also explores an International Patient Summary for travel and a later pharmacogenomics workflow under clinical oversight.
