Proposed integration

SATUSEHAT exchange

Evaluate FHIR R4 mapping and record continuity alongside existing reporting workflows.

Access across connectivity gaps

Work with local clinics to evaluate essential offline summaries and safe reconciliation when networks return.

BPJS claims verification

Explore whether encounter and provider references can help flag potentially inconsistent claims for human review in a separate evaluation.

Evaluation sequence

  1. Validate the exchange

    Use a development environment and synthetic encounters to check the mapping.

  2. Test offline workflows

    Measure snapshot freshness and staff handling of delayed updates.

  3. Assess later use cases

    Explore clinician-led screening workflows and telehealth record exchange with local partners.

An offline encounter to evaluate

Model a visit at a Puskesmas with intermittent connectivity. Staff would check whether a limited signed summary is still current, document the encounter and reconcile changes when the connection returns. A separate study would examine whether linking identity, provider, encounter and claim references could support human claims review.
