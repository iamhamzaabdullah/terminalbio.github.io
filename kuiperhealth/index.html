PROJECT STATUS

Early-stage concept

Kuiper Health is a proposal for portable clinical records. The first question is deliberately narrow: can a clinic verify a patient’s credential and retrieve the right record with that patient’s permission?

A referral, with the record attached

Imagine a patient referred to a second clinic. They authorize access in the app or present their card at the clinic. The provider verifies the credential and requests a permitted summary through Kuiper. The patient can review the access afterward.

This is the workflow the project intends to prototype. Clinical availability and country integrations remain future work.
