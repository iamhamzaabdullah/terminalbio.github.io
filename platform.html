A LIVING REPRESENTATION

Not a database.
A model of change.

Our platform represents biological state as a dynamic system. It integrates experimental evidence, molecular structure, sequence, and contextual data into a shared substrate that can reason about what happens next.

That means a result from one experiment becomes useful far beyond that experiment: it updates the causal hypotheses that guide every later decision.

THE AGENT LAYER

From insight
to action.

Intervention Engine searches the space of molecules, sequences, edits, and programs to find strategies that shift a system toward a desired state.

Experiment Engine translates uncertainty into a practical experimental plan—choosing the next measurement for what it teaches, not just what it produces.

Biological Agent coordinates the two: forming hypotheses, planning a sequence of tests, and adapting as new evidence arrives.
