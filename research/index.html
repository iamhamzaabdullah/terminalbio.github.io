THE RESEARCH THESIS

One biology.
Many scales.

Most biological systems are studied in fragments: a sequence benchmark here, a structural prediction there, an assay elsewhere. terminalBio develops models and tools that make those fragments more interoperable.

Our work is organized around a simple question: what representation would let an agent reason from a molecular observation to a biological decision with calibrated uncertainty?
