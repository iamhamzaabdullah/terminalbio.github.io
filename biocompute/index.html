01 / COMPUTATIONAL FOUNDATION

One system for the work
biology asks of computation.

Biocompute is terminalbio’s effort to make specialized biological models work as a connected research environment: grounded in evidence, reproducible in execution, and designed to learn from experiments.

Not another model in a box.

A biological question rarely fits inside one model. Biocompute is being developed as the computational layer that routes a question to suitable tools, carries its context through analysis, and keeps every result tied to how it was produced.
