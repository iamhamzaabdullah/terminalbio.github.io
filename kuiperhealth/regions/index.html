Regional integration studies

One core design. Two local pathways.

The Malaysia and Indonesia studies ask how portable records could work alongside existing health systems and reach clinics with intermittent connectivity.

Malaysia ↗

Explore a MySejahtera bridge, public and private care continuity, and workflows for rural clinics.

Indonesia ↗

Explore SATUSEHAT interoperability, offline access and a potential BPJS claims workflow.

These studies are design proposals. Implementation depends on local partners, authorization, technical feasibility and clinical evaluation.

What each study would test

Malaysia

Can a MySejahtera-compatible patient summary travel between a public clinic, a private provider and a virtual visit? Can a minimal summary still help when connectivity is limited?

Indonesia

Can a FHIR exchange work alongside SATUSEHAT reporting? Can community health centres use signed summaries safely when offline, and could verified encounter data support a separate BPJS claims review?
