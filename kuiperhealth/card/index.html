KUIPER HEALTH / HEALTH CARD

Kuiper Health Card

A physical credential for a digital record.

The proposed NFC card gives participating clinics a way to verify a patient credential and request the information they are authorized to see.

OverviewPlatformHealth CardPatient AppRegional studiesRoadmapPartnerships

What happens at a clinic

  1. Tap

    The patient presents the card to a participating clinic’s NFC reader.

  2. Verify

    The system checks the credential and the clinic’s authority to request a record.

  3. Retrieve

    The clinic receives the permitted record through the Kuiper API when connectivity is available.

Later work for low connectivity

Limited snapshots

The regional studies propose a signed snapshot of essential information for offline use. Its scope, expiry and storage protections would require testing.

Reconnect safely

A pilot would need to show how staff identify outdated snapshots and reconcile changes after connectivity returns.

What the card carries

The first card prototype would carry a cryptographic reference to the patient’s record. In the online workflow, the reader uses that reference to start verification and the provider retrieves only the permitted data. A later offline design would have to define what a signed snapshot can contain, how it expires, and what staff do when the card is lost or replaced.
