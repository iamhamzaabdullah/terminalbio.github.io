01 / THE COMPUTATIONAL FOUNDATION

Make the whole system
work together.

A prediction becomes useful when its inputs, assumptions, and experimental consequences stay connected. TB Biocompute is organized around four complementary capabilities.

01 / INTEROPERABILITY

Connect data and models.

Bring specialized biological models into a shared workflow. Keep sequence, structure, assay context, and model outputs connected so each stage can inform the next.

02 / REPRODUCIBILITY

Compare on common ground.

Make evaluation conditions explicit. Relate model versions, inputs, and assessment criteria to their outputs so comparisons remain interpretable and research can be revisited.

03 / TRACEABILITY

Keep the evidence attached.

Connect a candidate or prediction to the experiment that tests it. Preserve the readout and its context, including observations that challenge the original hypothesis.

04 / ORCHESTRATION

Build a continuous workflow.

Connect modeling, evaluation, candidate selection, and experimental feedback. This is the infrastructure direction behind terminalbio’s Biological Agent and world-model learning loop.

02 / FOLLOW A RESEARCH PROGRAM

From a question
to the next experiment.

Explore how the workflow fits together using binder discovery as a research example.

01

Define the question

Start with the biological target, the desired interaction, and the evidence available. Record the experimental context and the constraints that define a useful candidate.

02

Connect the right models

Use molecular interaction reasoning from BindFM and, for aptamer programs, generative design from XUZU. TB Biocompute provides the shared infrastructure direction for connecting these specialist roles.

03

Evaluate the candidates

Compare candidate outputs under explicit criteria. Keep model versions and assumptions attached to the ranking so the basis for prioritization remains visible.

04

Choose what to test

Translate the candidate shortlist into an experimental question. The Experiment Engine’s role is to prioritize measurements that can resolve consequential uncertainty.

05

Learn from the readout

Bring experimental outcomes back with their metadata. Use agreements and disagreements with predictions to inform the next evaluation, design, or hypothesis.
