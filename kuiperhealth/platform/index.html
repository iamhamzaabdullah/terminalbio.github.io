KUIPER HEALTH / PLATFORM

The Kuiper platform

Controlled access across care settings.

The proposed architecture connects clinical systems through HL7 FHIR R4 and pairs encrypted record storage with a permissioned audit layer.

OverviewPlatformHealth CardPatient AppRegional studiesMalaysiaIndonesiaRoadmapPartnerships

A proposed record exchange

  1. Structure

    A clinic maps an encounter to a minimal FHIR R4 record.

  2. Protect

    The clinical payload stays in encrypted storage. A permissioned ledger would track integrity references and authorization events.

  3. Authorize

    A provider requests a defined scope of access. The service checks the provider identity and whether the permission is still valid.

  4. Review

    Access events are recorded for review. Revocation applies to future requests and cannot recall a copy already received.

Questions the prototype must answer

Interoperability

Validate sample records and test whether a second clinical system can interpret them as intended.

Recovery and oversight

Test credential recovery, key rotation and oversight with clinical partners before handling real records.
