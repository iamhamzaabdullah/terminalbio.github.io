THE THREE-YEAR EXECUTION LAYER / 2026—2029

Focused work.
Compounding evidence.

XIRA3 translates the XIRA 2040 strategy into a focused research portfolio: rigorous synthesis, testable hypotheses, and original studies designed to strengthen experimental biology.

A defined first chapter26 planned outputs / four evidence tracks

PORTFOLIO DESIGN / 01

Review what is known.
Test what is missing.

Reviews map evidence and expose gaps; original research turns selected gaps into measurable questions. The portfolio spans senescence, organoids, cancer adaptation, neurobiology, RNA therapeutics, microbiome biology, and experimental control.
