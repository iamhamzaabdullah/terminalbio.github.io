TERMINALBIO RESEARCH STRATEGY / 2026—2040

Biology,
made programmable.

XIRA 2040 is our long-horizon research strategy: move from describing biological systems to predicting, testing, and ultimately engineering how they change.

A research program across scalesObserve → Predict → Establish causality → Perturb → Program → Engineer resilience

THE RESEARCH ARC / 01

From observation to controlled change.

Biology is dynamic, context-dependent, and shaped by interacting mechanisms. XIRA builds the evidence and experimental control needed to move from a snapshot of state toward reliable predictions about what a system will do next.

01 / INPUT

Observe

Measure states across time, space, and biological scale.

02 / REPRESENT

Predict

Model transitions, mechanisms, and uncertainty.

03 / TEST

Establish causality

Separate causal drivers from correlated signals.

04 / ACT

Perturb

Change a defined variable with temporal precision.

05 / DESIGN

Program

Combine interventions to steer a system toward a goal.

06 / OUTCOME

Engineer resilience

Seek stable, recoverable function—not a single readout.

ENABLING WORK / 02

Tools for asking better causal questions.

Optogenetics is one important control layer in this strategy: it can enable precise, reversible perturbations in suitable systems. It is an enabling method—not the identity of XIRA, and not a claim of near-term therapeutic readiness.

01 / CAUSAL CIRCUITS

Map mechanisms in context

Pair targeted perturbations with single-cell and spatial measurements to identify which relationships drive observed states.

02 / CLOSED LOOP

Measure, adapt, repeat

Connect optical actuators, biosensors, and live readouts so experiments can respond to the state of the system.

03 / TISSUE SYSTEMS

Control beyond the dish

Develop multiplexed control and organoid or tissue models where biological context makes the question meaningful.

04 / AI + EXPERIMENT

Put predictions at risk

Use models to propose state changes, then design experiments that can falsify those predictions.

STAGED ROADMAP / 03

Build capability in measurable steps.

Each stage earns the next. The sequence is a research direction, not a promise that every biological system will be controllable on schedule.

2026—2029

Foundation

Build robust model systems, baseline datasets, perturbation protocols, and reproducible measurement pipelines.

2029—2032

Integration

Connect optical perturbation to multiomic, electrophysiological, and spatial readouts in selected systems.

2032—2036

Experimental control

Test closed-loop and multiplexed control where actuator specificity, measurement quality, and system stability support it.

2036—2038

Programmable biology

Explore coordinated interventions that can reliably guide defined cellular or tissue states.

2038—2040

Frontier biology

Evaluate whether engineered control can produce resilient function across more complex biological contexts.

FLAGSHIP QUESTIONS / 04

Ten experiments that turn the thesis into evidence.

Flagships are concrete starting points for collaboration and iteration. They are questions to investigate, not results already established.

01

Optical causal mapping with single-cell multiomics

Connect targeted activity changes to cell-state responses.

02

Closed-loop stabilization of neural states

Test feedback control against defined electrophysiological state criteria.

03

Multiplexed control in neural organoids

Develop spatially and temporally resolved perturbations in human models.

04

Spatial multiomics after patterned perturbation

Resolve how local interventions reshape tissue neighborhoods.

05

Optogenetic control of intestinal organoid repair

Ask how defined signals affect regenerative trajectories.

06

Adaptive optical control with biosensors

Use measured molecular state to inform the next perturbation.

07

Light-addressable biological controllers

Engineer and characterize controllers with explicit operating limits.

08

Optical tests of AI-predicted state changes

Design perturbations that challenge model predictions causally.

09

Programmable neuroimmune interactions

Probe reciprocal signaling with cell-type and time-aware control.

10

From actuators to adaptive control

Link perturbation, sensing, and feedback into one testable system.

SCIENTIFIC GUARDRAILS

Any path toward translation must first establish delivery, specificity, safety, reversibility, and durable benefit. XIRA prioritizes rigorous causal evidence before therapeutic claims.
