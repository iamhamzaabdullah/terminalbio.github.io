Make the living

terminalBio is building the foundational intelligence layer for biology: systems that connect molecular detail to the experiments and decisions that matter.

OUR POINT OF VIEW

Biology is one
physics problem.

We believe molecular biology should not remain a collection of domain-specific benchmarks and disconnected experimental data. The most useful models will be the ones that can see the shared structure beneath it.

Our work brings model-building and experimental design into the same operating loop—because a model is only as useful as the questions it helps us ask next.
